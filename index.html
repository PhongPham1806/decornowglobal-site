Product tour

From the first message to money in the bank

HUB already runs in production for DecorNow's own sales and customer-service team, handling real conversations and orders every day.

Unified inbox

One screen for every conversation

Many Facebook pages, multiple personal Zalo accounts, Zalo OA, TikTok, Shopee, Lazada and web chat — routed to the right salesperson, with the customer's full profile beside the chat.

  • Priority, SLA and lead temperature on every thread
  • Customer profile, CRM stage and ad source in the side panel
  • Quick replies, media library and safe sending limits
AI for comments

Turn ad comments into conversations

AI reads every comment on Facebook posts and ads, understands the intent — price question, complaint, product question — and drafts both a public reply and a private message for staff to approve.

  • Intent detection with confidence score
  • Escalates angry or sensitive comments to a human
  • Approve and send in one click
AI Studio

Customers see the product in their own home

A customer sends a photo of their living room; HUB places the chosen artwork on their actual wall and sends the result back inside the same conversation — closing the sale faster.

  • Works from the customer's own photo
  • Uses the shop's real product catalogue
  • Delivered without leaving the chat
Orders & shipping

The full order lifecycle on one board

Create orders straight from the chat, book shipping with integrated carriers, track delivery and cash-on-delivery, and match incoming bank transfers to orders automatically.

  • Bulk actions and saved views for large order volumes
  • Integrated carriers with status and COD tracking
  • Automatic bank-transfer reconciliation and e-invoices
Products & inventory

Accurate stock, products and warranty

Products, variants, stock in and out, slow-moving item alerts and warranty tickets that are tracked until the item is returned to the customer.

  • Product catalogue synced to Facebook for ads
  • Import and export with Excel
  • Warranty cases with intake rules and status
Automation & broadcast

Follow-ups that run without people

If-then rules, a drag-and-drop automation studio with dry-run testing, follow-up sequences, after-hours bots and compliant bulk messaging across Facebook, Zalo and TikTok.

  • Dry-run every flow before turning it on
  • Respects each platform's messaging rules and consent
  • Approved message templates
